Water Level Indicators

Map icons represent flood severity—from no data to critical. Use these to quickly assess flood conditions in any area.

●

No Data

No flood data available for this location. Data collection in progress.

●

Low

Water levels are elevated but manageable. Minor monitoring recommended.

●

Moderate

Water levels are significantly raised. Prepare preventive measures.

●

High

Water levels are dangerously high. Immediate action recommended.

●

Critical

Severe flooding detected. Evacuate immediately if instructed.
